## Applicable

### 1. job.prompt
- Purpose: Caller-supplied generation instruction, free text and its structured form, rendered to wire text and bound to a prompt digest for reproducibility. It is the subject of the generation, so it cannot be minimised away, and nothing constrains a caller from describing an identifiable person in it.
- Retention: No automated deletion exists. The prompt is committed to job evidence and persists in ca-east-006 indefinitely; no guideline or procedure governs its destruction.
- Lawful basis: Collected to perform the generation the caller requested. The retention position is not defensible as written: the provision requires destruction once the identified purpose is fulfilled and requires documented procedures to govern it, and this product has neither.
- Citation: S.C. 2000, c. 5, sch 1, cl 4.5.3 (consolidated as of 2026-05-26; unofficial consolidation)

### 2. job.input_reference
- Purpose: Object-store key and optional digest for images supplied to the image-to-video and reference-to-video modes, up to four references per request. These are the most sensitive field in the product: an uploaded image can depict an identifiable individual, and the output is a moving likeness derived from it.
- Retention: No automated deletion exists. Input objects persist in the Canadian bucket under their prefix indefinitely.
- Lawful basis: ADR-002 expanded worker placement from three Québec regions to fourteen across six territories, so these images are now read by workers in Iceland, Norway, India, Japan and Australia. That is a communication of personal information outside Québec, which the provision requires be preceded by a privacy impact assessment weighing sensitivity, purpose and protection measures. No such assessment was conducted before the expansion.
- Citation: CQLR c. P-39.1, s 17 (consolidated as of 2026-04-01; unofficial consolidation)

### 3. job.output_video
- Purpose: The generated video committed to durable storage and served through a signed link. Where the input was a reference image of a person, the output is a synthetic likeness of that person.
- Retention: No automated deletion exists. Output objects persist indefinitely; only the download link expires.
- Lawful basis: Generation runs on rented third-party GPU capacity, so the personal information is transferred to a third party for processing and accountability for it remains with this organisation. The provision requires contractual or other means providing comparable protection during that processing. The credential model constrains what a worker may read and write, but no term of the provider arrangement has been assessed against this requirement.
- Citation: S.C. 2000, c. 5, sch 1, cl 4.1.3 (consolidated as of 2026-05-26; unofficial consolidation)

### 4. job.principal_id
- Purpose: Identifier of the calling principal, hashed with the request digest into the job identity and used to authorise result lookup and download. It is the only identity-shaped field the product holds.
- Retention: Persists for the life of the job record. It is an input to a SHA-256 job identity, so it is not recoverable from that digest, but it is held in configuration and passed to the delivery API in the clear.
- Lawful basis: This field is what makes a principal's records locatable, so it is the field an access request resolves against. The provision entitles an individual to be informed of the existence, use and disclosure of their personal information and to access it; no access-request path exists in this product.
- Citation: S.C. 2000, c. 5, sch 1, cl 4.9 (consolidated as of 2026-05-26; unofficial consolidation)

### 5. delivery.signed_link
- Purpose: Time-bounded URL issued for a committed video after the principal is reauthorised, so output is never served from a public object.
- Retention: Bounded by construction. The issuer caps TTL at 3600 seconds and rejects any request outside 1..max, and outputs default to private.
- Lawful basis: This is the one field whose safeguard is enforced rather than asserted. The provision requires protection against unauthorised access, disclosure and copying regardless of format, and a short-lived reauthorised link is a proportionate safeguard for the sensitivity involved.
- Citation: S.C. 2000, c. 5, sch 1, cl 4.7.1 (consolidated as of 2026-05-26; unofficial consolidation)
